# Governance — Paarvix

## Project Leadership

Paarvix is currently maintained by **Kannan / KannanTech** as the sole maintainer and Benevolent Dictator for Life (BDFL).

## Decision Making

- **Architecture decisions** are documented as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- **Feature requests** are discussed in GitHub Issues and Discussions.
- **The BDFL has final say** on all technical and strategic decisions.

## Becoming a Maintainer

As the community grows, trusted contributors will be invited to become maintainers based on:

- Consistent, high-quality contributions
- Understanding of the project's vision and principles
- Positive community interactions

The roadmap is defined in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) §13 and updated quarterly.
