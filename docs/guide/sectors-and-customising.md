# Sectors and Customising

> **Status:** today Paarvix connects to your data. Sector packs and the dashboard builder are planned. See the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). This page explains how they will work.

Paarvix is one product for every kind of organisation. It is built in three layers.

| Layer | What it holds | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Core** | Connections, queries, dashboards, alerts, roles, AI | Everyone |
| **Sector pack** | Ready-made dashboards, metrics, alert rules and labels | One sector |
| **Your setup** | Your data, column matching, users and branding | Your organisation |

## How sector packs fit your data

No two organisations name their columns the same way. A pack lists the fields it needs, such as `sale_date` and `amount`. Paarvix suggests a match from your columns, such as `Bill Dt` and `Net Amt`, and you confirm it. Your dashboards then appear in minutes.

## Example sectors

| Sector | Typical data | Dashboards | Who sees what |
|---|---|---|---|
| College | ERP export, Excel | Attendance, results, fees, placements | Principal sees all; HOD sees own department |
| Hospital | Hospital system database | Bed occupancy, OPD flow, pharmacy stock | Admin sees all; ward staff see their ward |
| Retail or SME | Excel, Sheets, MySQL | Daily sales, top products, stock, GST summary | Owner sees all; manager sees own branch |
| Factory | MySQL, sensors later | Machine status, downtime, output | Plant head sees all; supervisor sees own line |
| General company | Tally, Zoho, ERPNext later | Finance, sales, purchasing, HR | Management sees all; each team sees its own |

Paarvix shows what is happening in these systems. It does not replace your accounting or ERP software.

## How you can customise

No level needs code except the last one.

| Level | Who | What you can change |
|---|---|---|
| View | Anyone | Filters, date ranges, sorting, exports |
| Matching | Admin | Which of your columns fills each pack field |
| Dashboards | Editor | Add, move, resize or delete panels, and change charts and titles |
| Calculations | Analyst | Your own metrics, and SQL on databases |
| Rules | Admin | Alert thresholds, and who sees what |
| Branding | Admin | Themes, and your logo and white-label in Pro |
| Extensions | Developer | Custom connectors and panels through the plugin kit |

## Your changes stay yours

Applying a pack gives you your own copy of its dashboards. A later pack update cannot overwrite your changes. New versions are offered, and you choose whether to take them.

## Free and paid

Paarvix Community, including the core and basic packs, stays free. Premium packs, Pro features, paid setup and support are optional.
