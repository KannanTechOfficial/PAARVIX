# Welcome to the OmniViz Wiki

> **OmniViz** (powered by Paarvix core by [KannanTech](https://kannantech.com)) is an open-source, AI-native real-time data visualisation, analytics, and observability platform.

OmniViz is designed as a modern, lightweight, self-hosted alternative to Grafana and Metabase. It empowers small businesses, colleges, factories, and growing organizations to connect their existing data (Excel, Google Sheets, MySQL, PostgreSQL) and observe complex infrastructure (up to 10,000+ Linux & Kubernetes nodes) without needing an expensive dedicated data team or per-user licensing fees.

## 🌟 Core Pillars

1. **Simple by Default, Advanced on Demand:** Upload a spreadsheet and view data in 2 minutes; scale to multi-cluster telemetry when ready.
2. **True Columnar DataFrames:** Handles millions of time-series points without browser memory spikes or tab freezing.
3. **Sub-Millisecond LTTB Downsampling:** Downsamples 50,000 data points to 1,000 points in under 5 milliseconds while strictly preserving visual peaks and critical outliers.
4. **Bilingual AI Copilot:** Instant automated anomaly detection and executive summaries in both English and Tamil (தமிழ்).
5. **Ultra-Low Resource Footprint:** Runs fully on 2 GB RAM / 1 vCPU with less than 512 MB idle memory footprint.
